### **Jira Story: Add "Add to Cart" Button on Product Listing Page**

- **Issue Type:** Story
- **Epic:** Shopping Cart & Checkout
- **Priority:** High
- **Assignee:** Frontend Developer

#### **User Story**

> **As a** customer browsing the e-commerce website,
> **I want to** click an "Add to Cart" button directly on the product listing page,
> **So that** I can add items to my shopping cart quickly without needing to open each product's details page.

----

#### **Acceptance Criteria**

1. **Button Visibility:** An "Add to Cart" button must be displayed on every product card in the product listing/catalog view.
1. **Action & Feedback:**
  - When clicked, the item is successfully added to the user's shopping cart.
  - A visual indicator (e.g., a toast notification like *"Item added to cart!"* or a quick button state change to *"Added ✓"*) is shown to the user.
  - The cart icon counter in the website header updates dynamically in real-time to reflect the new item count.
1. **Inventory Handling:** If a product is out of stock, the button should be disabled and labeled as *"Out of Stock"*.
1. **Guest vs. Logged-in Users:**
  - For guest users, the cart items must be stored in local storage/session.
  - For logged-in users, the cart items must sync with the backend database.

----

#### **Technical Notes / Definition of Done**

- API endpoint `/api/cart/add` must be integrated.
- Unit tests written and passed for UI component states (In stock, Out of stock, Success state).
- Tested on desktop and mobile responsive views.
